# Commerce Readiness AI - Screenshots to Capture

Use this list when preparing GitHub, portfolio, or interview materials. Capture screenshots after running the app locally with the built-in sample dataset.

## 1. App Overview / Header

Show the app title, short intro, sidebar upload area, and main tab navigation.

Why it matters: Gives viewers immediate context for what the project is and how the workflow is organized.

## 2. Sample Data Loaded

Show the Product Data tab with the sample dataset visible and "Using: Sample data" shown.

Why it matters: Demonstrates that the project works out of the box without requiring a user upload.

## 3. Dashboard Metrics

Show the Dashboard tab with product, issue, affected-product, and average-score metrics.

Why it matters: Communicates the management-level summary value of the app.

## 4. Readiness Scores

Show the Scores tab with the multiple readiness score columns and review status.

Why it matters: Shows that readiness is explainable across data quality, marketplace, translation, compliance, and AI content dimensions.

## 5. Issues Table

Show the Issues tab with severity filtering and a mix of Critical, Warning, and Info issues.

Why it matters: Demonstrates the rule-based product data audit output.

## 6. Review Tasks

Show the Review Tasks tab with overview metrics, task filters, and the task table.

Why it matters: Shows how audit issues become practical operational work.

## 7. Review Workflow Status Override

Show the Manual Review Status Override section after selecting a product and a status.

Why it matters: Demonstrates the human review workflow without requiring a database or login.

## 8. AI Suggestions

Show the AI Suggestions tab. If no API key is configured, capture the missing-key fallback and prompt preview. If an API key is configured, capture one structured suggestion.

Why it matters: Shows the human-in-the-loop AI concept and that the app remains safe without an API key.

## 9. Management Export

Show the Management Export tab with the Management Summary Preview and download button.

Why it matters: Demonstrates the business handoff artifact for managers and operations teams.

## 10. Excel Export Opened

Optionally open `commerce_readiness_ai_management_export.xlsx` and capture the workbook tabs.

Why it matters: Confirms that the app output can be used outside Streamlit in a familiar business format.
