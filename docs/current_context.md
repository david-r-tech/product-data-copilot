## Current capabilities

- Fictional sample data or validated CSV/XLSX upload, with preserved text identifiers and limits of 10 MB, 1,000 rows, and 100 columns.
- Three workflow tabs: **Daten prüfen** leads with affected products and a single correction list; source data, five technical score components, and session-only manual status remain expandable. Explicit material, colour, and surface values are compared across dedicated columns and structured `attributes` entries; differing values are warnings for human review, not inferred facts from prose. **Texte erstellen & übersetzen** and **Ergebnisse & Export** cover drafts and the audit report. Deterministic checks and review-task export remain available, without presenting scores as proof of AI quality or compliance.
- **Texte erstellen & übersetzen:** a numbered path explains creation versus translation, article scope, language, request count, review, and download. For one product or the whole loaded table, create German HTML copy and up to five German bullets, then translate the complete text and bullets into English, French, or Spanish. Alternatively, translate an existing text column and optional bullet column. Additional supplier columns can inform the generated text. A reviewer selects each generated article, sees its source facts alongside the draft, and explicitly approves, rejects, or withdraws a decision.
- One user-triggered logical API request per product, with at most one provider retry; completed results remain in the session. Up to 100 products are processed per click, and further clicks continue larger files. The output is a draft requiring human factual review.
- CSV downloads, a five-sheet management workbook, and a separate two-sheet text workbook containing only approved texts and their matching source rows. Without approval, the text workbook is unavailable. Recognizable prices in the text workbook are numeric Excel cells; identifiers remain text. No source write-back.
- **189 automated tests** passed on 28 September 2026, including an offline OpenAI SDK request, individual approval, approved-only export, and workbook safety checks. Earlier live German-to-English creation requests for fictional `APP-001` returned valid JSON; the second confirmed factual copy and five translated bullets after tightening the prompt. A live translation-only request produced French text and two translated source bullets.
